# Code of conduct

SiliconPath welcomes learners and contributors of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and assume good intent; critique evidence and work, not people.
- Credit others' contributions and sources accurately.
- Help beginners; no question about fundamentals is unwelcome.
- Respect confidentiality, permissions and others' privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discriminatory or demeaning language, personal attacks, unwelcome sexual attention, publishing others' private information, and deliberately misrepresenting sources, results or affiliations.

## Enforcement
Report concerns privately to arun_gharami@live.com. The maintainer will review reports confidentially and may edit or remove contributions, or restrict participation, in proportion to the conduct.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
