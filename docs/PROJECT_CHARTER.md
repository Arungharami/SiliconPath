# SiliconPath project charter

## Identity
SiliconPath is an independent semiconductor education, planning and volunteer project-support initiative led by Arun Kumar Gharami. It is not presented as an accredited provider, incorporated company or institution-endorsed program.

## Mission and vision
Mission: connect credible learning, practical planning, digital tools and voluntary support.
Vision: a pathway from curiosity to knowledge, supervised capability and documented contribution.

## Audience
Learners, researchers, educators, workforce initiatives, lab planners and early industry project teams.

## Scope
Learning pathways; curated sources; conceptual lab models; planning and operational documentation; equipment comparisons; workforce mapping; bounded digital prototypes; pilot coordination.

Qualified specialists and the responsible host retain engineering, process-setting, installation, commissioning and safety responsibilities.

## Responsibilities
- Project lead: maintain the platform, coordinate agreed work, document sources and limitations.
- Host owner: define purpose, approve scope, provide permitted access and make project decisions.
- Contributors: accept bounded tasks, record evidence and hand over work.
- Reviewers: accept review individually and assess claims within their expertise.

No standing faculty or industrial partnership is claimed.

## Delivery cycle
Inquiry → capacity and fit assessment → agreed scope → delivery → review → handover.
Record deliverables, acceptance criteria, dates, owners, dependencies, costs, permissions and change requests.

## Proposed first pilot
A training-lab readiness study combining a scoped brief, requirements register, equipment/training comparison, one approved-data demonstration and a reviewed next-step report. A four-week schedule is a proposal subject to access and reviewer capacity.

## Success measures
Reviewable learning evidence; traceable requirements; host-accepted outputs; reproducible demonstrations; documented decisions and handover. No placement, certification, yield or factory-performance result is asserted.

## Current limitations
Resource full-content review, faculty validation, full browser QA and documented experience case studies remain pending. Shared accounts, cloud project journals and a dedicated organization namespace are not current platform capabilities.
