## Proposed course
Working title: Semiconductor Systems: PCB, Packaging & Digital Engineering.
Illustrative format: eight teaching blocks, adaptable by faculty. Course number, credits, eligibility, contact hours, delivery dates, fees and academic approval are not established.

1. Semiconductors in a system — Map chip → package → PCB → product. Output: An annotated system map.
2. Circuits and digital design — Review a simple circuit and its assumptions. Output: Simulation record and schematic.
3. PCB design workflow — Explore layout, interconnections and review criteria. Output: Preliminary board design review.
4. Assembly and inspection — Plan joining, inspection and traceable assembly records. Output: Virtual assembly worksheet.
5. Packaging and chiplets — Compare package roles, interconnects and thermal tradeoffs. Output: Source-backed packaging comparison.
6. Testing and evidence — Define measurements, units, repeatability and limitations. Output: Proposed test procedure.
7. AI and digital engineering — Evaluate a small model using clearly labeled synthetic data. Output: Reproducible baseline and ML comparison.
8. Capstone review — Present the design, evidence, open issues and next experiment. Output: Versioned portfolio and handover.

## Assessment proposal
20% learning evidence; 25% design and simulation; 20% packaging and test-planning study; 35% capstone. Faculty must review the weighting, workload and rubric before use. Completion does not confer a credential.

## Future labs
Lab 1: Digital Design, Simulation & AI.
Lab 2: PCB Assembly, Test & Packaging Development.
PCB assembly and semiconductor packaging are distinct capabilities. Physical advanced packaging fabrication requires a separate specialist and facility assessment.
