# SiliconPath
### Semiconductor education · Practical planning · Volunteer project support

[Explore the platform](https://siliconpath-six.vercel.app/) · [Mission & About](https://siliconpath-six.vercel.app/#about) · [Services](https://siliconpath-six.vercel.app/#support) · [Source repository](https://github.com/Arungharami/SiliconPath)

## Our mission
Connect credible semiconductor learning with practical planning, digital tools and voluntary project support so learners and project teams can make informed decisions.

## Our vision
A pathway from curiosity to capability: more people developing semiconductor knowledge, supervised practical skills and well-documented contributions.

## What we provide
| Area | Practical output |
| --- | --- |
| Learning | Guided pathways, original sources and study activities |
| Project readiness | Scope, requirements and open questions |
| Lab planning support | Concept workflows, equipment comparisons and review agendas |
| Workforce preparation | Skill maps, activity worksheets and training roadmaps |
| Digital tools | Bounded inventory, maintenance or knowledge demonstrations |
| Pilot coordination | Action tracking, decision records and documented handover |

## Start with a scoped pilot
Describe your goal, audience, project stage and requested output through the [project support page](https://siliconpath-six.vercel.app/#support). Volunteer capacity, responsibilities, reviewers and external costs are agreed before delivery.
