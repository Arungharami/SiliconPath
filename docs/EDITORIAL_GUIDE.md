# Editorial guide

For each item record title, author/organization, publication type, topic, prerequisites, level, access, original URL/DOI, date checked, suggested use and review state.

## Review workflow
1. Identified: candidate with source.
2. Source listing checked: metadata and relevance assessed; full text not yet reviewed.
3. Full-content reviewed: record reviewer, date, relevant pages/timestamps, methods/evidence and limitations.
4. Faculty reviewed: record faculty approval explicitly and the approved scope. Do not infer endorsement from informal feedback.

Every initial catalog entry is in state 2. Search retrieval confirms source listings, not that every live destination or full text was inspected. CLI checks validate syntax and record structure, not remote availability.

## Paper notes
Record problem, method, evidence, limitations, teaching application, full-text status and exact supporting pages. A workshop report or policy document should retain its publication type. Abstract-based notes must be labeled accordingly.

## Books and videos
Link to publishers or authorized open access. Specify the book edition and recommended chapters after review. Link/embed original videos only as permitted; add learning objectives and timestamps after viewing. Do not redistribute copyrighted files without permission.

## Training-center plans
Separate proposed equipment from confirmed inventory. Collect dated quotations and recurring operational requirements. Capacity depends on staffing, space, schedule and equipment. Checklists are user-entered progress, not institutional readiness certification. Process operation belongs under qualified local supervision and approved institutional procedures.
